# Wording examples

All examples assume the **eval fixture** résumé (Northline billing API, TypeScript/Node, 40k workspaces, 28% fewer failed-payment tickets) applying to Harbor, Senior Software Engineer. Do not copy these into live drafts for the real user.

## Cover letter

**Bad (generated):**

> I am writing to express my interest in the Senior Software Engineer role at Harbor. I am a passionate, results-driven engineer who thrives in fast-paced environments. I would leverage my extensive experience to deliver innovative, scalable solutions and would be a great fit for your mission. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how I can add value to your team.

**Good:**

> I'm applying for Senior Software Engineer at Harbor, on the billing/payments side of the posting. At Northline I own a TypeScript/Node billing API (Fastify, PostgreSQL) used by about 40k workspaces, plus a React admin for dunning. I'd be useful on webhook-heavy services and ops-facing tools, which is most of what this role describes.

## Why this company (short)

**Bad:** Harbor is at the intersection of fintech and delightful UX. I'm excited to join a world-class team unlocking the future of money.

**Good:** The posting is a senior IC on billing APIs and an internal React admin. That's the same shape of work as Northline's payments squad, which is why I applied.

## Describe a time

**Bad:** In a previous role I was tasked with improving a critical system. I collaborated cross-functionally, leveraged best practices, and delivered a robust solution that moved the needle.

**Good:** Failed-payment tickets were piling up on Northline ops. I shipped the dunning UI in React on top of the existing Node billing API and we saw 28% fewer of those tickets in 2023. That ops number is on my resume. It is not a revenue number.

## Additional information

**Bad:** Please find my résumé attached. I am happy to provide any further information and am available at your earliest convenience.

**Good:** Empty, unless something true is missing from other fields (for example: earliest start is 15 June, already in the profile).

## Sounds generated (punctuation, jargon, automation)

**Bad:** I genuinely enjoy building reliable backends; at Northline I owned billing — Fastify, PostgreSQL, React — and I’m honestly proud of that work.

**Bad:** This application was prepared to match the posting. As an experienced engineer I comprehensively delivered multifaceted solutions.

**Good:** At Northline I own the TypeScript billing API. We use Fastify and PostgreSQL. I also built the React admin for failed payments.

## Years of TypeScript (Number or short text)

**Bad:** I have extensive, production-grade TypeScript expertise across the full stack.

**Good:** `7` or `7 years` (2018–present on the fixture). Not 15. Not “expert”.
